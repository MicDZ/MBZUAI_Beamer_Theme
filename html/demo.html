Numbered Equations and Cross-References

Scaled dot-product attention vaswani2017attention maps queries $Q\in\R^{n\times d_k}$, keys $K$ and values $V$ to

\operatorname{Attn}(Q,K,V)=\softmax\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V .

Training minimises the expected negative log-likelihood

\begin{equation} \mathcal{L}(\theta) = -\E_{(\vx,\vy)\sim\mathcal{D}}\big[\log p_\theta(\vy\mid\vx)\big] + \lambda\,\lVert\theta\rVert_2^2 . \label{eq:loss} \end{equation}

Eq. \eqref{eq:attn} is used inside every layer, and the objective in is optimised with Adam\cite{kingma2014adam}.We use $\beta_1=0.9$, $\beta_2=0.98$ and $\epsilon=10^{-9}$.

Theorem, Definition and Proof

$f:\R^d\to\R$ is $L$-Lipschitz if $|f(\vx)-f(\vy)|\le L\lVert\vx-\vy\rVert$ for all $\vx,\vy\in\R^d$.

If $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $\eta\le 1/L$, gradient descent satisfies $f(\vx_{k+1})\le f(\vx_k)-\tfrac{\eta}{2}\lVert\nabla f(\vx_k)\rVert^2$.

Apply the quadratic upper bound $f(\vy)\le f(\vx)+\langle\nabla f(\vx),\vy-\vx\rangle+\tfrac{L}{2}\lVert\vy-\vx\rVert^2$ with $\vy=\vx-\eta\nabla f(\vx)$ and rearrange.
